## Protected behavior

execution-surface clarification, direct Workflow reading, and prompt-only
evidence limits. A later artifact generated in the Gemini App must receive its
own execution-target decision rather than inheriting an earlier direct-API
target. Protect that handoff and newly applicable App reading while allowing
different coherent portrait designs and replacement wording.

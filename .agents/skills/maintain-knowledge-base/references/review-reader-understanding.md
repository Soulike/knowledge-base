# Review reader understanding

Use this procedure for new or substantively changed Knowledge, Skills, and
Skill references selected by the parent workflow. Mechanical changes need
only evidence that meaning, structure, and required reader actions are unchanged.
Review the complete affected responsibility and its reading routes within the
agreed mutation boundary. Assessment-only work retains the parent's read-only
reporting boundary; these delivery checks do not authorize edits.

## Prepare independent observations

1. State the reader's task and necessary subject foundation. Include humans
   who know the subject but not the creation history, and Agents with different
   knowledge and comprehension abilities. Identify the final artifacts and
   the routes readers may use; keep authoring history out of the prerequisites.
2. Use a comparison model as the capability baseline for selecting a less
   capable reader. The reader checks whether the explanation supplies what it
   needs to understand and apply the content. Select and record both models using
   [Prove the behavior](../../../../knowledge-base/plugin/references/agents/skill-authoring.md#prove-the-behavior).
   Reuse its model-comparison, configuration, and evidence-recording rules for
   this understanding check.
   Unavailable applicable cross-model evidence leaves the check incomplete.
3. Reuse the parent workflow's independent suitability and semantic reviewer
   for the capable review when it can perform the examination below.
   A Skill's lower-capability behavior run may also supply the understanding
   evidence when it contains every applicable observation below. Add missing
   observations instead of repeating an otherwise sufficient run.

## Examine the explanation

4. Have the capable reviewer actively find hidden prerequisites, unexplained
   abstractions, and omitted reasoning. Trace who acts, what they act on,
   why the action follows, and what result or decision it supports.
   Check whether terms are explained before use, related or recurring terms
   are introduced early enough, and definitions avoid relying on other
   unexplained abstractions.
5. Check examples from the reader's starting knowledge. Establish the business
   situation and unfamiliar domain terms needed to understand each choice or
   result. Identify where an example or more explanation would resolve several
   plausible interpretations. Preserve intended meaning when proposing clearer
   wording or organization.
6. Give the lower-capability model the active instructions, reader task, final
   artifacts, and permitted reading routes in a fresh or isolated context.
   Withhold author conclusions, review findings, and expected answers.
   Ask it to explain the central rule and its reasoning in its own words,
   apply it to a realistic situation requiring a consequential distinction,
   and identify unclear terms, missing premises, uncertainty, or guessed meaning.
   A statement that the document is understandable is insufficient evidence.
7. Record the actual paraphrase, application, and reported confusion with the
   passages they concern. Compare them with the artifact's intended meaning
   and task requirements. Keep understanding, semantic correctness, and
   overall suitability as distinct conclusions. Agent observations provide
   evidence about these tasks, not proof that all human readers will succeed.

## Resolve and repeat

8. Investigate incorrect or hesitant responses before assigning a cause.
   Locate the relevant instruction or explanation and assess whether it
   supplies the necessary premises to a reader with the stated foundation.
   Check that assumed prerequisites follow from the reader's task.
   Use targeted, neutral follow-up tasks or another independent observation
   when one failure cannot distinguish a document gap from task demands beyond
   the model's necessary capabilities. Establish the cause from these
   observations before accepting or rejecting a document finding.
9. Write confirmed gaps into the authoritative reader-facing content within
   the authorized scope. Add prerequisites, concrete causal explanations,
   definitions, or contextual examples; reorganize the affected unit when
   scattered information prevents understanding. An explanation supplied only
   in a review reply does not repair the document.
   If a required repair exceeds scope, report it and the resulting limitation
   without silently expanding the change or claiming the issue resolved.
10. After repairs, repeat affected observations against the revised artifacts
    in a fresh context without the earlier answer or corrective coaching.
    Retain the observations and resolution in the PR or handoff work record.
    Finish only when required cross-model evidence exists and confirmed
    document gaps are resolved. Report unresolved material uncertainty as
    incomplete. For Skill behavior, retain the legitimate explicit capability
    and stopping paths defined by the linked behavior-evidence rules; those
    paths do not substitute for missing understanding observations.

# Collaboration Handbook

This Handbook supplies required understanding for collaboration. It does not
own the procedure or completion of the user's task. Apply its packaged guidance
as supplemental to instructions, requirements, Skills, and project-specific
decisions in the active working directory.

## Foundation

At the beginning of every task, read
[Independent collaboration](../../references/agents/independent-collaboration.md)
in full before planning or acting. Apply it throughout the task alongside every
task-specific Skill and other applicable guidance.

Re-establish this foundation after interruption, compaction, or resumption when
the complete earlier reading is no longer available. Reread it when the
reference changed or its necessary basis cannot otherwise be established.

If the complete reference cannot be read, report the gap and how it limits the
next dependent step. Preserve decisions that depend on the missing guidance,
continue independent work that does not depend on it, and restore the reading
route when possible. Do not invent missing instructions or claim that reading
completed.

The foundation is established only when the complete reference has been read
and remains available for the current work. Loading this Handbook does not
complete the user's task or create a separate approval step.

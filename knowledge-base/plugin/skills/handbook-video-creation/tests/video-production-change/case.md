# Re-evaluate video packages as production changes

## Identity

- ID: video-production-change
- Kind: journey

## Covered objects

- [Video creation Handbook](../../SKILL.md)

## Protected behavior

The video Handbook has no forced foundation. A non-AI people-focused continuous
shot whose production medium and style are both undecided must read only the
general continuous-shot package. When source frames and an anime production
method appear later, image-to-video and anime-animation guidance must accumulate
without loading live-action design.

## Task

Plan one continuous shot centered on a dancer as a non-AI preproduction concept.
We have not decided whether it will be filmed or animated, and the visual style
is also undecided. After the shot plan is established, use supplied opening and
closing frame descriptions to prepare an anime-influenced image-to-video version
of the same action.

## Starting state

Supply the natural first request, current real candidate metadata, and normal
access to selected Skill bodies and direct packaged routes. After the initial
selection, package choice, reads, and shot plan are captured, provide concise
opening and closing frame descriptions and reveal the anime image-to-video
request. No video service call is permitted; the final artifact is a portable
prompt and shot specification.

Use a fresh supported lower-capability model with the expanded executor guard
and a bounded read-only allowance. Keep evaluator-only materials private.
Record source identity, blind selection, every package choice, observable reads,
the initial shot plan, later re-evaluation, and the final specification.

## Expected behavior

- V1 (critical): select `handbook-collaboration` and
  `handbook-video-creation` from the initial natural request before body access.
- V2 (critical): for the initial non-AI concept, read people-focused
  continuous-shot design without forcing image-to-video, photographic-video, or
  anime-animation guidance while production medium and style remain undecided.
- V3: apply the general shot reading to continuity, motion, staging, and
  evaluable intent rather than prematurely choosing a production medium or
  visual style.
- V4 (critical): when source frames and anime generation appear, re-evaluate
  and cumulatively add image-to-video prompting and people-focused anime
  animation while reusing the current general continuous-shot reading.
- V5: exclude photographic or live-action video design in the anime phase and
  preserve the source-frame contract in the portable result.

## Verification

Capture blind native selection and the initial package choice before showing
expected packages. Inspect reads and the shot plan, then introduce the source
frames and changed production method and capture supplementary reading and the
final artifact. Give the capable reviewer this contract and raw observations
without the coordinator's verdict. Judge selection, package choice, reading,
application, and re-evaluation separately.

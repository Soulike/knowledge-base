# Deliver the documentation

Use this procedure only for authorized delivery selected by
[Select the task path](../SKILL.md#select-the-task-path).

## Write and reconcile

1. Apply the selected structure and matching project standards to write each
   resolved target as one clear current account. Complete the necessary rewrite
   within the authorized responsibility boundary regardless of diff size, while
   preserving meaning outside the accepted semantic change.
2. Apply the loaded Knowledge's boundary between authoring evidence and
   reader-facing content. Treat research, source collection, review evidence,
   rejected alternatives, drafts, and task history as working inputs; carry
   them into a maintained target only when they serve its reader-facing
   responsibility.
3. Reconcile every affected document, comment, example, index, prompt, Agent
   instruction, Skill, link, and navigation entry. When a documented invariant
   changes, reconcile its enforcement and evidence as well.
4. When the composed result, new requirements, or feedback invalidate the
   structural judgment, return
   to the main workflow's
   [response and structure decision](../SKILL.md#decide-the-response-and-structure)
   before further editing.

## Verify the result

1. Account for every changed file, changed meaning, information need, impact
   finding, and affected artifact. Confirm that each has the recorded
   disposition.
2. For every new or changed claim, record the assertion, its owning source and
   location, and the meaning and scope that source establishes in the work
   record. Distinguish intended contract, observed implementation behavior, and
   inference; for an inference, identify the evidence and reasoning that support
   its substance. Names or observed value propagation alone do not establish
   undeclared behavior or intent. Declarations and passing examples establish
   only the facets they actually define or exercise. Compare the final wording,
   including what it teaches a reader to do or expect, with that source-based
   account. Retain a claim only when evidence supports its substance and scope;
   qualifiers may express supported uncertainty but cannot supply missing
   evidence or turn an unsupported assertion into a valid interpretation. Omit
   unsupported factual or instructional claims and report the evidence gap,
   validation limit, or required project decision. Do not invent meaning to make
   the document appear complete. Leave delivery incomplete when the remaining
   gap prevents the promised reader result. Confirm that every described claim
   agrees with its authority in meaning and scope, every mandatory mechanism has
   a basis beyond its desired outcome, and examples preserve choices the
   underlying contract permits.
3. Apply the loaded Knowledge's
   [suitability review](../../../knowledge/documentation/maintained-document-quality.md#validate-the-result)
   and risk-proportionate reader validation to the complete final content and
   required routes. Record the reader task, relevant locations, and outcome
   separately from semantic correctness. Return an unsuitable result to the
   main workflow's structure decision before claiming delivery complete.
   Confirm that no unexplained competing copy
   or authoring-only material remains.
4. Check every changed route from the reader's starting point. Confirm its label
   or condition lets the reader choose the destination before opening it.
   Search for stale terminology, paths, headings, examples, prompts, and
   superseded prose.
5. Run the project's formatter, link or anchor checks, Skill validation, and
   other applicable checks. Treat a failed or unavailable required check as an
   incomplete result.

Return the final impact and affected-artifact account, suitability review,
reconciled routes, and check results to
[Report and complete](../SKILL.md#report-and-complete).
